## Anti-Patterns Verdict

Not classic AI slop. The editor has real product bones: a coherent workflow, chart-first intent, meaningful data review, and serious settings depth. The risk is not "AI made this"; it is "prototype grew into a workstation without enough editorial compression."

Main slop tells: tiny-label density everywhere, repeated accordion/control stacks, improvised symbolic buttons, and several "everything is available" panels where the product should make a stronger judgment.

## Overall Impression

DataCanvas is already more real than most MVP chart tools: import, review, direct canvas editing, undo, annotations, export, and many chart controls are all present. The single biggest opportunity is to stop treating the editor as a settings catalog and make it behave like a chart-first inspector.

## What's Working

1. The four-step model is right: source, review, chart, design maps cleanly to the user's mental model.
2. Data review is unusually strong: issue counts, column inspector, duplicate handling, type conversion, and edit affordances create trust.
3. The editor has serious professional ingredients: direct selection, annotations, selected series/element editors, undo/redo, and export.

## Priority Issues

**[P1] The design panel is too much panel for one job**

Why it matters: The product promise is "fast path with depth," but the right panel exposes publication-depth settings before establishing the fast path.

Fix: Split styling into "Essentials" and "Advanced" modes. Essentials should cover canvas preset, title/subtitle, palette, labels, axes, and export. Advanced gets custom fonts, exact ticks, annotations, individual elements, and deep text styles.

Suggested command: `$impeccable distill /editor`

**[P1] Chart-first principle is weakened by symmetrical side chrome**

Why it matters: The left chart picker, center stage, and right settings panel compete as equal regions. The chart should feel like the object being made, not the middle column of a dense IDE.

Fix: In design mode, collapse chart picker into compact chart/data summary. Give canvas more width and make the right panel contextual. Keep the full chart picker in the chart-selection step.

Suggested command: `$impeccable layout /editor`

**[P2] Design-system drift is already visible**

Why it matters: Detector found 148 undocumented colors, 40 undocumented radii, and 6 font findings. Some are legitimate tonal variants, but the volume means future UI work will keep drifting unless tokens or CSS usage get tightened.

Fix: Add deliberate semantic tonal tokens for common hover/status/focus colors, add missing radius steps or normalize to the documented scale, and replace app-shell Arial usage with the documented type system unless chart export intentionally needs it.

Suggested command: `$impeccable extract /editor`

**[P2] Microcopy explains controls, but does not guide decisions**

Why it matters: Labels are accurate, but users need recommendation logic at high-risk moments: aggregation, missing values, critical data issues, direct labels, and export format.

Fix: Add decision-oriented hints only where users can make misleading charts. Remove low-value helper text elsewhere.

Suggested command: `$impeccable clarify /editor`

**[P2] Visual language is strict but over-fragmented**

Why it matters: The brand wants editorial restraint, but the implementation uses many small symbolic marks and button idioms. It feels functional, not fully designed.

Fix: Standardize icon language with one icon set or fewer symbols. Replace ambiguous glyph buttons with known icons plus accessible labels/tooltips.

Suggested command: `$impeccable polish /editor`

**[P3] Data warnings allow forward motion without enough ceremony**

Why it matters: Continuing with critical data issues is flexible, but a novice may produce a misleading chart.

Fix: Keep the escape hatch, but make the primary action state explicit: "Continue anyway" when critical issues exist, with a concise reason and visible warning count.

Suggested command: `$impeccable harden /editor`

## Persona Red Flags

**Power User**: Undo/redo, direct canvas selection, and keyboard undo are strong. Red flags: settings quick nav is a dropdown, no visible search/command palette, advanced controls are buried in accordion geography.

**First-Timer**: Upload and data review are clear. Red flags: after chart choice, the design panel's density spikes; symbols require interpretation; critical data warnings do not create enough friction.

**Student Analyst**: Demo datasets and chart categories help. Red flags: chart choice is a gallery, not guidance; styling controls invite tinkering before the chart's message is clear; export appears only in design mode.

## Minor Observations

- The persistent "Шаг X из 4" marker is useful but visually close to the tiny uppercase kicker pattern.
- The close editor `×` feels risky without destination/context.
- 7-10px labels risk fatigue and contrast problems.
- "Снять выделение" may be better as Escape behavior plus a subtle selected-object chip.
- Custom font upload is powerful but too prominent for most chart-making sessions.
- Test fixture and chart-export font findings should not be treated as app-shell failures without intent review.
