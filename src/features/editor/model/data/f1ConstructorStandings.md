Five completed seasons, ten teams, positions 1–10. These are supplied final
positions, not rankings calculated from points. In particular Alfa Romeo
and Aston Martin both scored 55 in 2022, but finished sixth and seventh.

Series use abbreviated 2025 names, with two explicitly documented lineages:
Sauber = Alfa Romeo (2021–2023), Kick Sauber (2024–2025).
Racing Bulls = AlphaTauri (2021–2023), RB (2024), Racing Bulls (2025).

One constant team color is used for the whole series, not each historical
livery. Eight colors were read from --f1-team-colour in the current official
team page (https://www.formula1.com/en/teams). Haas uses its darker familiar
silver, and Sauber uses its pre-Audi green, to remain legible and avoid giving
historical Sauber records the color of the 2026 Audi rebrand.
