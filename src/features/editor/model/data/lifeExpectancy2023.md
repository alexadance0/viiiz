The map example uses the 2023 country and territory observations from
[Our World in Data: Life expectancy](https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/life-expectancy).
The measure is period life expectancy at birth, in years, for both sexes.
The 2023 estimates originate from UN World Population Prospects (2024),
processed by Our World in Data.

Downloaded from the Grapher CSV on 2026-10-04. The JSON records the download
URL and SHA-256 of the complete CSV. It retains all 237 rows with ISO alpha-3
country codes or OWID_KOS for 2023, at the source precision. World, continental,
income and other group aggregates are excluded. Territories without a map
polygon retain their rows; missing values are never replaced by zero.

The example requires no external requests at runtime. Map geometry and tile
grid attribution remain documented in the map data README.
