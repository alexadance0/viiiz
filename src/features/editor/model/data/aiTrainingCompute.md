All 536 systems with publication dates and positive training computation are
retained, without sampling, aggregation or imputation. Dates span 1950-07-02
to 2026-08-07. Only system name, date and computation are bundled. Numerical
precision is retained. Dates are instantiated as local calendar dates.

Computation is total petaFLOP (10^15 floating-point operations), not FLOP/s.
These are literature-based estimates, with uncertainty: typically within a
factor of 2, or 5 for recent undisclosed models such as GPT-4. The chart uses
a logarithmic Y axis; source values are not logarithmically transformed.
